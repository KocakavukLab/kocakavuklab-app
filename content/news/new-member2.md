**Marco Tembrink** joins as a UMEA Clinician Scientist, focusing on genomic characterization of CNS lymphomas.

**Jiawei Zhou** joins as a Visiting Researcher from Jiangsu Cancer Hospital, working on real-world cancer datasets.

**Dr. Quan Shi** joins as a Postdoctoral Researcher, funded by the DFG, focusing on the development and application of single-cell and spatial transcriptomics technologies in cancer research.

We are excited to have these talented researchers join our team!
