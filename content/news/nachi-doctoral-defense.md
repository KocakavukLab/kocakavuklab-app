We congratulate our lab manager, **Dr. Sc. Hum. Nachiyappan Venkatachalam** (*Doctor scientiarum humanarum* / Doctor of Human Sciences), on successfully defending his doctoral thesis on **16 October 2026** at the **Medical Faculty Mannheim, Heidelberg University**.

His thesis, **“Mechanisms of AXIN1 Loss upon MEK Inhibition in Colorectal Cancer,”** examined crosstalk between **Ras–MAPK and Wnt signalling**. He carried out his doctoral research from **October 2021 to October 2026** in the **Department of Medicine II, University Medical Center Mannheim**, under the supervision of **Prof. Dr. med. Tianzuo Zhan**.

The research investigated how MEK inhibition reduces levels of **AXIN1**, a key regulator of Wnt signalling. Experiments in colorectal cancer cell lines and organoid models identified **mTOR-associated suppression of protein synthesis** as a mechanism of AXIN1 loss, helping explain how Ras–MAPK signalling regulates AXIN1 abundance.

These findings were published in **Cell Communication and Signaling (2026)**: [Ras-MAPK inhibition induces AXIN1 loss in colorectal cancer by mTOR associated suppression of protein synthesis](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12964-026-02963-4).

His additional publications include [Combined MEK and PARP inhibition enhances radiation response in rectal cancer](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2025.102284) in **Cell Reports Medicine (2025)**, [Cross-Talk between p53 and Wnt Signaling in Cancer](https://doi.org/10.3390/biom12030453) in **Biomolecules (2022)**, and [Predicting response to neoadjuvant chemoradiotherapy in rectal cancer: from biomarkers to tumor models](https://doi.org/10.1177/17588359221077972) in **Therapeutic Advances in Medical Oncology (2022)**.

His doctoral research was supported by **Collaborative Research Centre SFB 1324, “Mechanisms and Functions of Wnt Signaling.”**

Congratulations, Nachi, on this milestone! 🎉
