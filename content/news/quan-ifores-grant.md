We congratulate Dr. Quan Shi, our Alexander von Humboldt Fellow, on receiving the IFORES Career Kickstart Grant, the University of Duisburg-Essen's internal research funding programme (Interne Forschungsförderung Essen). The award provides €49,500 in funding to support his research project.

[Read more about the funded projects](https://www.uni-due.de/med/forschung/forschungsfoerderung/career_kickstart_gefoerderteprojekte.php)
