Our collaborative study, **“Divergent genomic evolution in astrocytomas and oligodendrogliomas,”** was published in **Nature on 14 October 2026**.

The GLASS Consortium compared tumour genomes over time in **183 patients with IDH-mutant glioma: 94 with astrocytoma and 89 with oligodendroglioma**.

At recurrence, **oligodendrogliomas acquired new driver mutations**, including alterations in PI3K-pathway genes and NOTCH1. **Astrocytomas accumulated DNA copy-number changes**, including oncogene amplifications and CDKN2A/B deletions, alongside increasing chromosomal instability.

Temozolomide exposure was associated with mutational signatures in both tumour types; a radiotherapy-associated signature increased specifically in astrocytomas. These observational findings motivate research into tumour-type-specific treatments. They do not establish clinical benefit, and the repeat-surgery cohort may not represent all patients.

**Emre Kocakavuk is a co-first author**, sharing equal first authorship with Tamrin Chowdhury and C. Mircea S. Tesileanu. Kocakavuk Lab members **Pranav Swaroop Gundla and Cihat Karadag** also contributed to this GLASS Consortium study. The findings add to our understanding of how these tumour types evolve over time.
